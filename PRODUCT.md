# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Studio.** Small-business owners, entrepreneurs, and founders evaluating help with a website, web application, branding, or motion work. Some arrive through networking, referrals, or a business card. They need clear service descriptions, examples of finished work, and a way to describe a project.
- **Portfolio.** Hiring managers, recruiters, product and engineering teams, and prospective collaborators evaluating Jona Ferreira’s product design and UX engineering work. They need to see ownership, decisions, implementation, and results, with a résumé and contact details within reach.

## Product Purpose

DevignUX is Jona Ferreira’s website, with two equally important destinations under one brand. Studio helps prospective clients assess fit and make a qualified inquiry. Portfolio helps people evaluate Jona’s work and decide whether to hire or collaborate with him. The root page helps visitors reach the destination that matches their intent.

## Positioning

Jona brings product design and engineering to the same engagement, from understanding the problem through building and launching the result. DevignUX shows that connection through shipped work and detailed case studies that explain the decisions behind it.

## Operating Context

- Studio at `/studio` supports both direct referrals and visitors who discover DevignUX on the web. Its services, process, project showcase, and inquiry form help visitors move from evaluating the work to sharing a brief.
- Portfolio at `/portfolio` supports quick evaluation and deeper reading. Selected Work provides project previews. Dedicated case studies explain context, contributions, decisions, and delivered work.
- Visitors can enter either destination directly. Preserve existing routes, section links, and navigation between the two experiences.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Studio’s four service areas are Websites, Web Applications, Branding & Marketing, and Motion & Video.
- Studio inquiries collect project and contact details. Portfolio offers a résumé download, direct contact, and project demonstrations.
- Use approved screenshots and local demonstrations for portfolio proof. The enterprise case studies use reconstructed interfaces, and Donor Directory uses fictional donor records. Protect proprietary materials and keep demonstrations isolated from live client systems.

## Brand Commitments

Use **DevignUX**, **Studio**, and **Portfolio** consistently. Write in plain language about the work, the people it serves, and what was delivered. Keep the established identity and content of each destination when refining it.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Mirembe Cleaning.** The Studio showcase and case study use approved desktop and mobile captures. The case study documents a multilingual website, a booking-request journey, the first completed customer cleaning, and additional booked appointments. See `src/data/mirembeCleaningCaseStudy.ts` and `public/mirembe-case-study/`.
- **Templates Manager and Donor Directory.** The enterprise case studies use interactive reconstructions to explain ownership and sharing, donor discovery, and the design decisions behind those workflows. See `src/data/projects.ts` and `src/data/donorDirectoryCaseStudy.ts`.
- **Professional materials.** The Portfolio includes Jona’s introduction, résumé, and contact links. See `src/pages/PortfolioPage.tsx` and `src/config/site.ts`.

## Product Principles

1. Give Studio and Portfolio equal standing and give each audience a clear route to the evidence and next step it needs.
2. Show the work before making claims. Use approved materials and verified outcomes, and distinguish delivered capabilities from measured results.
3. Explain individual responsibility and collaboration so readers can understand how the work moved from discovery to delivery.
4. Carry accessibility and performance through to the shipped experience, including previews and demonstrations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, meaningful text alternatives, and logical reading order. Support narrow screens, touch input, and reduced-motion preferences. Keep essential information and actions available without relying on animation.
